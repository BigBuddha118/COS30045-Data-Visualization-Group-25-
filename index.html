Three things the data shows

Breath testing has not bounced back

In 2020 every state and territory tested fewer drivers. National breath tests fell from 15.2 million in 2019 to 7.3 million, and by 2024 they had recovered only to 10.3 million, still 33% below 2019.

Speeding makes up most fines

Of the 3.89 million fines issued for four offences in 2024, 85.5% were for speeding. Mobile phone use made up 8.5%.

Cameras changed mobile phone fines

In 2024, cameras detected about 91% of New South Wales, 96% of Queensland and 99% of ACT mobile phone fines. Fines in those places rose sharply.

Read the numbers with care

These figures count what police do — tests carried out and fines issued — not how many people break the law. More fines can mean more offending, more police activity, or new technology such as cameras. Each chart is followed by a note on what it can and cannot show, and the About the Data page explains where the figures come from and where they are incomplete.
